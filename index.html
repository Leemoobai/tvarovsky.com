— Main Services

Four ways I can be engaged.

I.

Arbitrator

Sole or panel arbitrator under GAFTA, FOSFA, and LMAA rules. Accepting appointments and nominations in grain, oilseed, and shipping disputes — including contract performance, charter party, quality, and payment claims.

II.

Expert Statements &
Market Price Assessments

Independent expert reports on market price, trade custom, and commercial practice. Statements prepared for use in arbitration, litigation, and commercial negotiation — grounded in active market knowledge.

III.

Trade Representative

Representation of principals in the physical commodities and shipping markets. Supplier and buyer introductions, price discovery, contract negotiation, and execution oversight. Assistance with arbitration claims, submissions, and procedural correspondence. Trade finance instruments including Letters of Credit and CAD.

IV.

Consultancy & Advice

Pre-dispute advisory, contract review, risk mitigation, and trade execution strategy. GAFTA/FOSFA contract drafting review, Incoterms advice, and operational consulting for trading desks and shipping principals.
